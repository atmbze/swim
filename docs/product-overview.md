# Product overview
SOWESTIM is South West Item Mart LLC's regional marketplace for the US Southwest.
- **Marketplace**: anyone browses; registered users sell (listings with inventory and a photo), buy now, or make offers.
- **Local discovery**: filter by city text or by distance from a known Southwest ZIP (offline centroid table).
- **Storefronts**: `/sellers/:id` shows a seller's profile and listings.
- **Messaging**: 1:1 buyer–seller conversations, optionally tied to a listing.
- **BuyBacker**: users submit items; SWIM admins accept with a quote or reject.
- **Deal Den**: percentage promo codes (site-wide by admin, or seller-scoped / listing-scoped), with expiry and max uses.
- **Moderation**: users flag listings; admins remove/restore listings and ban/unban users.
